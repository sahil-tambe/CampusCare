ABOUT CAMPUSCARE

Making campus a better place, one return at a time.

Losing personal belongings on campus can be stressful. CampusCare provides a common platform where students can report lost and found items, discover possible matches and reconnect belongings with their owners.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple steps. Meaningful returns.

01. Report

Submit details about an item you've lost or found.

02. Discover

Search listings and explore potential item matches.

03. Verify

Submit a claim and verify ownership through the platform.

04. Recover

Complete a safe handover and reunite with your belongings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some things you might want to know about CampusCare.

Students with an eligible Somaiya institutional email address can register and use CampusCare.

Register or log in, open the lost-item reporting section, and provide the relevant item details.

Submit a claim with identifying information. The recovery process includes ownership verification.

CampusCare is designed to protect personal information. Private ownership evidence and sensitive details are not publicly displayed.

CampusCare is designed as a free campus lost-and-found platform.
